Welcome to the Student Clubs & Events Home Page! The student clubs and events organizers serve as your hub for student life. We aim to connect students with a vibrant community of clubs and organizations, as well as student-led events. These events take place both on and off campus an are designed to help student discover their passions, connect with new people, and make the most of their professional and social goals while attending CCSU.

Whether you're a student looking to lead, learn, or simply searching for new and exciting expereinces, the Student Clubs & Events organization has something for everyone!
